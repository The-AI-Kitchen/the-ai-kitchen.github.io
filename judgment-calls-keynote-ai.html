Judgment Calls While Building a Keynote with AI

David Coad recently used AI to develop a keynote speech for a conference: bouncing ideas around, exploring slide designs, and creating images that express the emotion behind his thoughts. He also leaned on colleagues, friends, and his own judgment to make the presentation work. In this session, he walks through how to bring AI and human judgment together when developing an oral presentation.

Friday, October 16, 2026 · 1:00–3:00 p.m.
California Mission Room · Benson Memorial Center 036 (basement), Santa Clara University

What David will show

Follow the choices behind a real keynote, from developing ideas to deciding what belongs in the final presentation.

  • Brainstorm with AI. Use a chatbot to bounce ideas around as you develop a speech.
  • Explore slide designs. Work with AI to find ways to present your ideas visually.
  • Create and refine images. Iterate on visuals that express the emotion behind what you want to say.
  • Bring in human judgment. Combine feedback from colleagues and friends with your own decisions about what makes the presentation effective.

Before you come

Bring a laptop. It helps to have a presentation topic in mind so you have something to explore during the hands-on portion.

Have your AI tools ready

David uses Claude through Perplexity, but the workflow is tool agnostic. Use tools you can access that support both of these activities:

  1. Chatbot brainstorming: discuss and develop ideas for a presentation.
  2. Image generation: create an image and refine it through three or four consecutive iterations.
Sign in and check that you can use both capabilities before the session. You may use one tool or separate tools for brainstorming and images. See the AI Tools Guide for SCU Students for options.
