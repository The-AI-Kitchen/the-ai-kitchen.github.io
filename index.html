Before you stop by

A few good questions.

Still wondering about something?
Say hello.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. AI Kitchen welcomes every major and every level of experience. The Friday tutorials are designed to give you a place to start.

Do I need to sign up?

No signup is required for Friday Taste Tests. Just show up. You can join the update list to hear about upcoming sessions.

What should I bring?

A laptop and a little curiosity. Check the session details and tools guide for any suggested setup before you arrive.

Can I come if I’m not an SCU student?

Yes. AI Kitchen is open to the public, including faculty, staff, and community members.
