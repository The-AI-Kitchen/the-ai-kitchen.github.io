How Fridays work

Every session starts with a live tutorial from our guest at 1:00, followed by a hands-on build for everyone in the room and time for open experimentation and questions. Most Fridays have a soft finish around 4:00, and you’re welcome to stay and keep trying things together. Sessions marked 1:00–3:00 wrap at 3:00 sharp so the room can turn over to the next event.

Want to be a guest chef? Dates marked “Speaker to be announced” are open. Read the guest chef guide or email klukoff@scu.edu.
