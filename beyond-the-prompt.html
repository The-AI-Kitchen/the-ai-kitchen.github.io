Beyond the Prompt: Getting Real Work Done with AI Agents

How I operate as a non-profit CEO in my spare time

Friday, November 13, 2026 · 1:00–3:00 p.m.
California Mission Room · Benson Memorial Center 036 (basement), Santa Clara University
Directions to Benson 036

About the session

What happens when you give AI a project instead of a question? Adam Smith (SCU ’08), CEO of Bear Valley National Ski Patrol, shares how he collaborates with his personal AI assistant, Kepler, on volunteer leadership, community responsibilities and home projects, including the useful results, wrong turns and decisions that still need a human. After a demonstration, participants will practice the same approach in a hands-on exercise and leave with a framework for deciding what to delegate, what to verify and when to step in.

Part of the AI Kitchen Taste Test series at Santa Clara University.
