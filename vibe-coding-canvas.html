Vibe Coding Canvas: Claude Cowork as a Recipe for Students and Teachers

Jeremy Kemp will lead a session on Claude Cowork with three concepts you can use in your own work: use an Air Gap, write a Policy as Code, and install an Agent designer. Explore working locally with data from Camino and building teaching and learning content directly in Camino.

Friday, October 2, 2026 · 1:00–4:00 p.m. · soft finish
California Mission Room · Benson Memorial Center 036 (basement), Santa Clara University
Directions to Benson 036

Three concepts to try

Jeremy will cover three concepts you can use in your own work.

  • Use an Air Gap. Extract your data package from Camino and stir it into a useful dish locally.
  • Write a Policy as Code. Craft your own personal guardrails for your Agent in Camino.
  • Install an Agent designer. Build directly in Camino — teaching and learning content.
