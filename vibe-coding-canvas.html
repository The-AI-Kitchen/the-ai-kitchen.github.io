Mise en place: get ready to help

Adapted from Jeremy’s guide for the October 2 session. Choose one of four recipes below, or help a participant get started. Students and teachers can use Camino (Canvas); staff can use a PDF or materials from their own work.

  1. For students

    1. Make an academic integrity skill

    Combine the SCU academic integrity pledge, your syllabus’s AI policy, and your personal boundaries into integrity.md.

    Turn those instructions into a reusable skill, save it, and test it on an academic task.

  2. For teachers & staff

    2. Make a commitments skill

    Use a syllabus or workplace guidance to create commitments.md: what AI can help with, which decisions you keep, and when approval is required.

    Save it as a reusable skill and test whether it flags a task that conflicts with your commitments.

  3. For teachers

    3. Export and audit a course

    In your course, go to Settings → Export Course Content → Course → Create Export → New Export.

    When the export is ready, download the .imscc file and extract a copy (rename the copy to .zip if needed). Ask AI to flag confusing instructions, missing resources, and inconsistencies.

    Canvas course export help →

  4. For students

    4. Turn coursework into a portfolio

    Go to Account → Settings → Download Submissions → Create Export → New Export.

    Unzip the download, choose two or three pieces, and draft LinkedIn-ready descriptions of your contribution and skills. Verify the descriptions and credit collaborators before posting.

    Canvas submission download help →

Save your guardrails

  • Claude for Mac: In Cowork, open + → Record a skill. Review and save the result. Claude skill help.
  • ChatGPT for Mac: Select Work or Codex, then open Plugins → + → Record a skill. Demonstrate your workflow, stop recording, and review the drafted skill. Recording requires Computer Use to be enabled. ChatGPT recording help.
  • Keep the instructions handy: Save and enable the skill, then add a memory or preference: “Before academic or work tasks, consult my guardrail skill and flag conflicts.”

Quick help for participants

  • No desktop app? Download your files and upload selected materials to a browser chat, along with your guardrail instructions.
  • Need a syllabus PDF? Open the page, then choose Print → Save as PDF. Canvas page-to-PDF help.
  • No Camino or Canvas? Use a PDF, report, instructions, or other workplace materials.
  • Privacy or policy question? Remove sensitive information before uploading or recording, and work on copies.
