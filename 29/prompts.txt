2. HAVE A REAL CHATGPT VOICE CONVERSATION

Start Voice, allow microphone access if asked, and speak this instruction. Use headphones if available; speak quietly or take turns with a partner. Voice is a back-and-forth conversation, separate from the microphone that only dictates text into the composer.

"I’m going to think out loud about my interests and what I might want to explore at SCU. My ideas may be scattered or pull in different directions. Help me make sense of them. Respond with what you’re hearing, useful questions, or possible directions. I’ll react and we’ll refine the direction together. Don’t research or build the field guide yet."

Speak freely: interests, half-formed ideas, examples, constraints, and competing directions are all useful. Listen to its response, react to ideas, correct misunderstandings, and add context. Go back and forth for a few minutes. When finished, return to the chat. Typed fallback: use the same conversational approach in text.

3. READ AND CORRECT YOUR BRIEF

"Turn our conversation into a short interest brief: interests, goal, preferences, constraints, and open questions. Separate what I told you from your suggestions. Keep it to a short paragraph. Wait while I read and correct it."

Check in: Correct an assumption or add a missing detail. Keep a copy before leaving Temporary chat. Copy only your reviewed brief into a new normal ChatGPT Work task in the next step. Explicitly use the supplied brief and public sources only.
