Recipes to take home

A website involves choices about who it is for, what it should say, and what matters most. We will explore how to make those choices visible as you work with AI, and when a quick request is enough. The same approach can help with presentations, research, and other projects where a good result depends on context and judgment.

  • Understand how AI agents work. See how software connects a model to tools and a workspace so it can act, inspect results, and revise. Explore how reasoning and feedback guide those steps.
  • Interview me first. Ask AI to help clarify a fuzzy goal, your audience, and the choices you still need to make before it starts building.
  • Choose the right amount of planning. A quick prompt can be enough for a clear, bounded task with adequate context. When important choices are unresolved, make room for questions and a plan.
  • Give AI context it can use. Bring source materials, examples, and your preferences together in a brief you can reuse. This is context engineering.
  • Keep human judgment in the loop. Inspect the result, decide what to keep, and steer the next revision with specific feedback.

Try it yourself

Use a website idea to practice the process: a personal page, a project portfolio, or a page for a club, lab, program, or event. The goal is to learn a way of working with AI that you can reuse, whether or not you finish or publish the page.
